# Privacy & Legal Compliance Audit

## Executive Summary

A privacy and regulatory audit was conducted for Wright-Way Services LLC per `WEBSITE_PRODUCTION_STANDARDS.md` (Section 5).

---

## Compliance Evaluation

### 1. Visitor-Facing Legal Documents
Publicly accessible modal dialogs integrated into the website footer cover:
- **Privacy Policy**: Details data collection (Name, Email, Phone, Project Location), purpose (quote processing), non-disclosure policies, and contact information.
- **Terms of Service**: Outlines service scope (home inspection & remodeling), quote validity, intellectual property, and jurisdiction (South Carolina).
- **Cookie Policy**: Clarifies zero third-party tracking pixels or profiling cookies, explaining minimal essential web storage use.
- **Accessibility Statement**: Documents WCAG 2.1 AA compliance measures and accessibility feedback contact methods.

### 2. Data Minimization
- No user tracking pixels, intrusive analytics cookies, or cross-site behavioral tracking scripts are installed.
- Form submissions collect only essential contact and project parameters required to evaluate service requests.
